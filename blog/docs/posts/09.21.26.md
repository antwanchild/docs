# Long Time No See...

<!-- more -->

I flew into Salt Lake City to spend the week working in South Jordan, Utah, onboarding and helping train Jennifer.

I was especially excited about the possibility of driving to Provo that evening for a fireside with Kimberly Jo Smith. I had met her 25 years earlier while serving as a missionary for The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints in the Oklahoma Tulsa Mission. At the time, I was assigned to the Ava Ward in Missouri.

The fireside was at a young single adult (YSA) ward in Provo. Kim is a great-great-granddaughter of the Prophet Joseph Smith. She had remarried since I last spoke with her in Ava.

I hadn't made the drive to Provo in a while. Despite the rush hour traffic, the trip was uneventful. I felt peaceful and excited as I drove. I arrived at the stake center a little early and walked around outside for a few minutes until people started arriving. When a vehicle pulled up, I spotted someone I thought was Kim, though I didn't recognize her new husband.
