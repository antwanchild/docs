# Long Time No See...

<!-- more -->

I flew into Salt Lake City to spend the week working in South Jordan, Utah, onboarding and helping train Jennifer.

I was especially excited about the possibility of driving to Provo that evening for a fireside with Kimberly Jo Smith. I had met her 25 years earlier while serving as a missionary for The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints in the Oklahoma Tulsa Mission. At the time, I was assigned to the Ava Ward in Missouri. Kim had been a member of the Church for only about two years, having converted from the Reorganized Church.

The fireside was at a young single adult (YSA) ward in Provo. Kim is a great-great-granddaughter of the Prophet Joseph Smith. She had remarried since I last spoke with her in Ava.

I hadn't made the drive to Provo in a while. Despite the rush hour traffic, the trip was uneventful. I felt peaceful and excited as I drove. I arrived at the stake center a little early and walked around outside for a few minutes until people started arriving. When a vehicle pulled up, I spotted someone I thought was Kim, though I didn't recognize her new husband.

She got out of the car, and we looked at each other. Then I heard her call out, "It's Elder Child!"

We visited for a few minutes, and I learned that her two children, Leah and Bryan Davis, had been sealed to her and her husband, Steve.

As the fireside began, Kim spoke about how wonderful it was to see some of her friends from Nauvoo. She also mentioned that one of her missionaries was there and referred to herself as my "missionary mom."

She shared stories about Emma Smith's conversion and why she stayed behind. The fireside really touched me, and I learned things I had never known before. Kim had done much of that research in the years since we first met.
