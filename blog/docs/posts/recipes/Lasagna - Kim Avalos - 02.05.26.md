# Lasagna - Grandma's Recipe

It's non-traditional, and it's the best!

<!-- more -->

{{ recipe_header() }}

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 lb Italian sausage
- 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce (approximate)
- 1/2 onion, diced (optional)
- 1 box lasagna noodles
- 1 package frozen spinach
- 2 lbs mozzarella or Monterey Jack cheese, shredded
- 2 cans cream of mushroom soup
- 3/4 can milk
- 1 small can tomato sauce
- Oregano, to taste
- Salt, to taste
- Parmesan cheese, to taste
- Garlic salt, to taste
- Dried parsley, to taste

## Instructions

1. Brown the ground beef and sausage together. If using onion, cook it with the meat. Add approximately 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce, adjusting to taste.
2. Cook the spinach according to the package directions and add it to the meat mixture once the meat is cooked through.
3. Cook the lasagna noodles and drain. Pat them dry with paper towels.
4. Heat the cream of mushroom soup with 3/4 can of milk. It does not need to be hot, just warm enough to become smooth and creamy.
5. In a greased lasagna pan, add one layer of noodles, then half of the meat mixture and half of the cheese. Repeat with a second layer.
6. Slowly pour the soup mixture over the top of the lasagna. Swirl the tomato sauce over the top.
7. Add Parmesan cheese, garlic salt, dried parsley, and oregano to taste.
8. Cover the pan with foil and bake at 350°F for 30-45 minutes, until bubbly.

!!! tip "Recipe Note"
    Let the lasagna rest for a few minutes after baking before slicing. It will be easier to serve and gives everything a chance to settle.

**Enjoy!**
