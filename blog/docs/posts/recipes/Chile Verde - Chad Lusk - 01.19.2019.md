# Chile Verde

This Chile Verde is off the hook!

<!-- more -->

{{ recipe_header() }}

## Ingredients

- 5 lbs thick, boneless pork chops, cut into chunks
- Vegetable oil
- 1 tbsp ground cumin
- 1 tbsp salt
- 2 tbsp flour
- About 1 cup water
- 6 cans roasted, diced green chiles
- 1 can roasted, diced jalapeños
- Salt, to taste
- Ground garlic, to taste

## Instructions

1. Fry the pork in vegetable oil until the released liquid has cooked away and browned bits form on the bottom of the pan.
2. Add 1 tbsp ground cumin and 1 tbsp salt.
3. Sprinkle in 2 tbsp flour.
4. Cook for a couple of minutes, turning frequently and scraping the browned bits from the bottom of the pan.
5. Add about 1 cup of water and keep the mixture simmering.
6. Add the roasted diced green chiles and roasted diced jalapeños.
7. Season to taste with salt and ground garlic.
8. Simmer for about 15 minutes before serving.

!!! tip "Recipe Note"
    The Chile Verde is good fresh, but the flavor gets even better if you give everything some time to mingle.

**Enjoy!**
